Our Story

FROM SMALL BEGINNINGS TO GLOBAL EXCELLENCE

Founded in 1998 in Jaipur, Rajasthan, SteelForge Industries started as a small fabrication shop with just 12 employees. Today, we are a 5,000+ strong organization with 12 manufacturing facilities across India, serving clients in 45+ countries.

Our journey has been defined by relentless innovation, commitment to quality, and an unwavering focus on customer satisfaction. We have grown into one of India's most trusted industrial manufacturing companies.
